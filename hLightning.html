Help Lightning

iOS Developer at Help Lightning, Inc. • Beijing, China

Project Overview & Highlights

Engineered an interactive B2B remote assistance iOS application merging two real-time video streams, allowing field technicians and remote experts to collaborate visually in real-time.

  • Developed real-time video state management and contact synchronization pipelines.
  • Implemented encrypted local data storage utilizing Realm, SQLCipher, and Objective-C/Swift bridges.
  • Integrated Firebase notification services and real-time session signaling.
