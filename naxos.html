Software Architect at NWN Corporation • Beijing, China

Architectural Summary

Architected and launched the mobile application for Naxos Music Library, enabling high-performance audio streaming for over 100,000 CDs and 1.5M+ music tracks to subscribers globally.

  • Engineered low-latency audio buffering and playback controls using AVFoundation and custom caching.
  • Implemented full playlist creation, offline licensing validation, and catalog search indexing.
  • Built cross-platform client architecture using Xamarin, Objective-C, and REST Web Services on AWS.
